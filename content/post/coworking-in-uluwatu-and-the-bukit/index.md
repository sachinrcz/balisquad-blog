## Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit: Working From Bali's Southern Cliffs

For a long time the Bukit was where you came to surf and switch off, not to get work done. That has changed. Uluwatu, Pecatu and Ungasan now have proper coworking spaces with fibre internet, 24/7 access and real communities, and you can still be at a cliff-top sunset an hour after your last call.

This guide covers the coworking spaces on the Bukit, what they cost, and how to make remote work here actually work. For rents, getting around and the nearest hospitals, see our [Uluwatu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/uluwatu).

---

### What makes working from the Bukit different

A few things to know before you pick a base:

- **Distances are longer than they look.** Uluwatu is spread out across the peninsula. Almost everything needs a scooter or a car, so choose a space close to where you sleep.
- **Power and water cuts happen now and then.** Ask your villa and your coworking space about backup power.
- **It is quieter than Canggu.** Fewer cafés and fewer spaces, but also less traffic, and the community tends to know each other by name.

## Coworking spaces in Uluwatu and the Bukit

### BukitHub Coworking (Ungasan)

BukitHub started when two nomads built the office they wanted to work from every day, then opened it up to the community. Alongside the focus floor there is a gaming zone, a meeting room and social breakout corners.

- **Where:** Jl. Pura Batu Pageh No.177AA, Ungasan
- **Access:** 24/7 keycard access for weekly and monthly members
- **Setup:** fibre internet, 2K external monitors, height-adjustable standing desks, a meeting room for up to 8 people, a game room, printing, and free coffee, tea and water
- **Day pass:** IDR 150K (50% off on your first visit)
- **Flexi pass:** IDR 1.0M for 10 coworking days within 30 days
- **Weekly pass:** IDR 600K, with a dedicated desk and 1 hour of meeting room time a day
- **Monthly pass:** IDR 2.0M, with a dedicated desk, mail handling and 1 hour of meeting room time a day
- **Meeting room:** IDR 100K per hour

The community side is the real draw: language classes, tech talks, AI workshops, board games, badminton and padel. For a taste, read our recaps of the [second Cursor meetup at BukitHub](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/cursor-bali-meetup-june-2026/) and [AI Coworking Meetup #8](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/ai-coworking-meetup-8/).

Best for: long-stay nomads and builders who want a focused desk and a ready-made social circle in the quieter southern Bukit.

### BWork Uluwatu (Pecatu)

BWork's second site after Canggu is a big one: four floors, more than 200 workstations, 10+ meeting rooms, 15+ focus rooms and private offices. The wellness side is just as large, with a pool, bouldering wall, gym, Pilates room and yoga shala.

- **Where:** Jl. Labuansait, Pecatu
- **Access:** coworking is open 24/7; the café runs 8 am to 9 pm
- **Day pass:** Rp 299K (Rp 199K for KTP holders)
- **Weekend day pass:** Rp 200K
- **One week unlimited:** Rp 1,200K
- **One month unlimited:** Rp 3,850K, which also covers BWork Canggu
- **Non-peak monthly:** Rp 2,000K for 3 pm to 10 am access

BWork Uluwatu also hosts community sessions like Coffee & Connect, which you will often find on our [Uluwatu events page](https://balisquad.com/event/in/uluwatu).

Best for: people who want a large, all-in-one space and like to train where they work.

### Uluwatu Hub (Pecatu)

A hybrid coworking and coliving space set in a garden, with a pool, an open-air gym, a café, focus rooms and meeting rooms. All coworking memberships include the pool and gym.

- **Where:** Jalan Goa Lempeh No.27A, Pecatu
- **Access:** coworking is open 24/7; the café runs 8 am to 10 pm. It closes for important Balinese holidays such as Nyepi.
- **Internet:** advertised at 1 Gbps
- **Day pass:** from IDR 200K. Day passes are walk-in only and can't be booked ahead, so arrive early.
- **Longer stays:** for weekly and monthly prices, check [uluwatuhub.com](https://uluwatuhub.com/)

Best for: people who want to live and work under one roof.

## How much does coworking in Uluwatu cost?

Our [Uluwatu guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/uluwatu) puts a monthly coworking pass at roughly **IDR 2.5M to 4M**. Here is how the spaces above compare:

| Option | Price |
|---|---|
| Cheapest day pass | IDR 150K at BukitHub (75K on your first visit) |
| Full-service day pass | Rp 299K at BWork Uluwatu |
| Part-time (10 days a month) | IDR 1.0M Flexi pass at BukitHub |
| Monthly with a dedicated desk | IDR 2.0M at BukitHub |
| Monthly unlimited with a pool and gym | Rp 3,850K at BWork (includes Canggu) |

Prices change and promos come and go, so check each space's own site before you pay.

## Where to base yourself on the Bukit

The Bukit is not one place. Choose the pocket that fits your days:

- **Ungasan:** quieter and more residential, close to Melasti and Pandawa beaches, and a short ride to Nusa Dua and Jimbaran. We wrote about its slower side in [Hidden Gems of Uluwatu: Discover the Charm of Bukit, Ungasan](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/hidden-gems-of-uluwatu-discover-the-charm-of-bukit-ungasan/).
- **Pecatu, Bingin and Padang Padang:** closer to the famous surf breaks and the busier café strip along the main roads.
- **Jimbaran:** a calm bay between the airport and the Bukit, and a good middle ground if you often go into the city. See the [Jimbaran guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/jimbaran).
- **Nusa Dua:** calm swimming beaches and a resort area, good if you are here with family. See the [Nusa Dua guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/nusa-dua).

## Tips for remote work in Uluwatu

**1. Don't rely only on villa Wi-Fi.** Ask your host for a speed test screenshot before you book, and keep a mobile data plan as backup.

**2. Plan around the sun.** The Bukit is at its best early in the morning and at sunset. Many people work late morning to mid-afternoon at a space, then head out.

**3. Get a scooter you are comfortable on.** Roads here are hilly and spread out. Our [scooter rental guide](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/renting-a-scooter-in-bali/) covers licences, helmets and fair prices.

**4. Know where to go if something goes wrong.** Surf cuts and scooter scrapes happen. Our [clinics and hospitals guide](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/clinics-and-hospitals-in-bali/) lists the 24-hour options near Uluwatu.

**5. Use coworking to meet people.** The Bukit community is smaller than Canggu's, which makes it easier to become a regular. Go to one event a week and you will start seeing the same faces at the beach.

## FAQ: Coworking in Uluwatu

**Is there good coworking in Uluwatu?**
Yes. BukitHub in Ungasan, BWork Uluwatu in Pecatu and Uluwatu Hub in Pecatu all offer dedicated workspaces with 24/7 access for members.

**What is the cheapest coworking in Uluwatu?**
Of the spaces listed here, BukitHub has the lowest day pass (IDR 150K, half price on your first visit) and an IDR 2.0M monthly pass.

**Can I use one membership in Canggu and Uluwatu?**
BWork's one-month unlimited plan (Rp 3,850K) covers both its Canggu and Uluwatu locations.

**Is Uluwatu or Canggu better for digital nomads?**
Canggu has more spaces and a bigger scene. Uluwatu is quieter, closer to world-class surf, and its community is smaller and tighter. See [Coworking in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/) to compare.

**Do I need a scooter to get to coworking in Uluwatu?**
Usually, yes. Distances on the Bukit are long and walking is rarely practical. Ride-hailing works but can take longer to arrive in quieter spots.
