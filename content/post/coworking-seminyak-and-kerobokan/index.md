## Coworking Seminyak, Kerobokan & Umalas: where locals actually work

Seminyak is where a lot of Bali remote workers eat, shop and meet — but the best desk is not always on Eat Street. Sunset Road and the quieter strips into Kerobokan and Umalas hold most of the serious coworking spaces, plus cafés that double as offices on light days.

This guide lists places in **Seminyak, Kerobokan and Umalas** that we could verify are operating, with a straight take on vibe and only prices or hours we found on official sites. If you are still picking a neighbourhood, start with our [Seminyak area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/seminyak) and [Kerobokan area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/kerobokan).

For other parts of the island, see [Coworking in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/) and [Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/).

## Coworking spaces in Seminyak & Sunset Road

Sunset Road is the spine between Seminyak, Legian and Kerobokan. Most “Seminyak coworking” searches land here — close to villas and hotels, but away from beach traffic.

### Biliq Seminyak

Biliq’s flagship is the quiet, design-led space many long-term nomads mention first: indoor AC, outdoor bale seating, a dipping pool, pantry, and a soundproof Skype room for calls.

- **Area:** Seminyak (Jl. Yudistira)
- **Vibe:** Calm and focused; feels more like a boutique office than a social hub
- **Hosting hours (walk-ins / flexi):** Monday–Friday 9:00–18:00; no hosting on weekends ([Biliq Seminyak](https://www.biliqbali.com/coworkingspaceseminyak))
- **Members:** 24/7 access (same source)
- **Pricing:** Flexidesk from IDR 500 per minute ([Biliq Bali](https://www.biliqbali.com/))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Biliq+Seminyak+Jl.+Yudistira+No.+3A+Seminyak)

Best for: deep work days and video calls when you want a polished space near Seminyak’s restaurants.

### Biliq Sunset

Biliq’s second location on Sunset Road leans slightly more formal, with more dedicated desks and private offices — plus virtual-office services in the same building.

- **Area:** Sunset Road (between Seminyak and Kuta)
- **Vibe:** Professional; good if you commute along Sunset Road or need a business address
- **Hosting hours (day pass / first timers):** Monday–Friday 9:00–17:00; no weekend hosting ([Biliq Sunset](https://www.biliqbali.com/coworkingspacesunset))
- **Members:** 24/7 access (same source)
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Biliq+Sunset+Ruko+Sunset+Indah+II+Sunset+Road+Kuta)

Best for: teams and nomads who want Biliq quality with easier access to the airport and Denpasar.

### Work Hub Coworking Space

Work Hub opened on Sunset Road as a comfortable, gym-equipped coworking space aimed at freelancers, remote employees and small teams.

- **Area:** Seminyak (Sunset Road)
- **Vibe:** Modern and practical; mix of common area, focus rooms and meeting space — reviews often mention reliable Wi‑Fi and helpful staff
- **Hours (daily / weekly / monthly packages):** 9:00–18:00 ([Work Hub daily package](https://workhub.co.id/daily-package/))
- **Included:** Common area, focus room, pantry and gym access on day passes (same source)
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Work+Hub+Coworking+Jl.+Sunset+Road+85+X+Seminyak)

Best for: a full-day pass when you want AC, a focus room and a workout in the same building.

### Kembali Innovation Hub (vOffice)

KE{M}BALI is a multi-storey innovation hub on Sunset Road — coworking desks, meeting rooms and event space, operated in partnership with [vOffice](https://voffice.co.id/en/locations/kembali-innovation-hub).

- **Area:** Seminyak (Sunset Road)
- **Vibe:** Corporate-creative; popular with startups and local teams; less “barefoot nomad” than Biliq
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday 8:00–18:00 ([vOffice Kembali Innovation Hub](https://voffice.co.id/en/locations/kembali-innovation-hub))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kembali+Innovation+Hub+Jl.+Sunset+Road+28+Seminyak)

Best for: meetings, workshops and desk time when you want a proper business building near Seminyak.

### Bali Bustle Sunset

Bali Bustle’s Sunset Road property is coliving-first, with coworking tied into the same ecosystem as their other Bustle locations (one membership can span sites — see their [coworking overview](https://balibustle.com/coworking-space-bali/)).

- **Area:** Sunset Road (between Seminyak and Legian; property listing: Jl. Sunset Road No. 68)
- **Vibe:** Friendly, community-led; pool, café and gym energy — good if you like meeting people in-house
- **Coworking day pass (Bali Bustle network):** IDR 150,000 common space day pass ([Bali Bustle coworking](https://balibustle.com/coworking-space-bali/))
- **Coworking access:** 24/7 for members (same source)
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bali+Bustle+Sunset+Jl.+Sunset+Road+68+Legian)

Best for: staying and working on Sunset Road with one bill and a built-in social scene.

### Sunset Coworking

Sunset Coworking focuses on **furnished office rentals** (including yearly setups) for teams that need stable internet and IT support — not a drop-in café vibe.

- **Area:** Seminyak (Sunset Road — Jl. Beji Ayu)
- **Vibe:** Business setup and serviced offices; suited to companies landing in Bali rather than casual hot-desking
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sunset+Coworking+Jl.+Beji+Ayu+6+Seminyak)

Best for: teams that need a long-term office with furniture and connectivity handled for them ([Sunset Coworking](https://www.sunsetcoworking.com/)).

## Coworking spaces in Kerobokan

Kerobokan sits north of Seminyak and west toward Umalas — villa neighbourhoods, local warungs, and a growing strip of work spaces along Jl. Raya Kerobokan and Jl. Raya Semer.

### Ranjana Café & Coworking Space

Ranjana pairs a café with proper coworking tiers: open workspace, private desks, phone booths and meeting rooms.

- **Area:** Kerobokan Kelod
- **Vibe:** Café energy downstairs, more structured upstairs; good for hybrid “coffee then desk” days
- **Hours:** Weekdays 8:00–20:00; weekends 8:00–18:00 ([Ranjana coworking](https://ranjanacoffee.com/coworking-space-kerobokan/))
- **Pricing:** Private desk / table from IDR 30,000; private office from IDR 500,000; meeting room from IDR 150,000 (same source)
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ranjana+Cafe+Jl.+Merta+Ayu+20+Kerobokan)

Best for: affordable desk time without committing to a monthly membership.

### Daily Hub Bali

Daily Hub is a café and coworking space on Jl. Raya Semer with indoor and outdoor work areas and bookable meeting rooms.

- **Area:** Kerobokan (Semer)
- **Vibe:** Bright and casual; popular for meetings and creative sessions
- **Hours:** Daily 8:00–21:00 ([Daily Hub Bali](https://dailyhubbali.com/))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Daily+Hub+Bali+Jl.+Raya+Semer+23+Kerobokan)

Best for: a long café day with meeting-room backup when you need a screen or whiteboard ([facilities](https://dailyhubbali.com/our-facilities/)).

### G88 Coworking Space

G88 is a large, multi-level coworking venue on Jl. Raya Kerobokan — known for spacious tables, long hours and an adjacent gym in the same building complex.

- **Area:** Kerobokan Kelod
- **Vibe:** Big-floor energy; can feel lively (and reviews mention occasional noise from events or construction). Better for heads-down solo work than sensitive client calls
- **Hours:** Daily 7:00–24:00 ([G88 listing — Serviced Office](https://www.servicedoffice.com/indonesia/bali/badung-regency/g88-coworking-space))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=G88+Coworking+Jl.+Raya+Kerobokan+88+Kerobokan)

Best for: night-owl schedules and nomads who want gym access nearby without a separate membership.

### Meet me Bali (The Lounge)

Meet me Bali runs a café at Pro Padel Bali with **The Lounge** upstairs for coworking, meetings and events.

- **Area:** Kerobokan Kelod
- **Vibe:** Social and polished; coffee-roastery crowd; good when you want food and desk in one stop
- **Hours:** Café and The Lounge daily 8:00–22:00 ([Meet me Bali](https://meetmebali.com/))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Meet+me+Bali+Jl.+Raya+Kerobokan+264+Kerobokan)

Best for: meetings or coworking sessions with excellent coffee on tap.

## Coworking & work-friendly spots in Umalas

Umalas is the green, villa-heavy belt between Seminyak and Canggu — quieter roads, fewer tour buses, and a handful of places worth knowing if you stay north of Petitenget.

### Nomad Hub Bali

Nomad Hub is a coliving property with an on-site coworking floor and Nomad Café — positioned for guests who want pool, room and desk in one place.

- **Area:** Umalas / Kerobokan (Jl. Bumbak)
- **Vibe:** Quiet, residential; rooftop-style work area with natural light (guest reports); more “stay here and work” than drop-in café
- **Nomad Café hours:** Daily 9:00–17:00 ([Trip.com property listing](https://www.trip.com/hotels/bali-hotel-detail-81294689/nomad-hub-bali/))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nomad+Hub+Bali+Jl.+Bumbak+22+Kerobokan)

Best for: coliving stays between Seminyak and Berawa; confirm day-pass access with the property before you ride over.

### ROU Coffee & Coworking

ROU is a Tokyo-inspired specialty coffee shop that has offered coworking since 2016 — garden seating, indoor tables and a calm Umalas neighbourhood feel.

- **Area:** Umalas
- **Vibe:** Quiet, creative, Japanese coffee-house polish; good for solo focus and small meetings
- **Hours:** Daily 7:00–16:00 ([ROU Coffee](https://www.roucoffee.tokyo/what-we-serve/))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ROU+Coffee+Jl.+Dukuh+Indah+Umalas)

Best for: morning blocks and early calls before Seminyak traffic picks up.

## Laptop-friendly cafés (no day pass required)

On light days you might only need good coffee, power and a table. These are **cafés**, not coworking spaces — buy food and drinks, keep calls short, and avoid peak brunch if you need a table for hours.

### Expat. Roasters Seminyak Petitenget

Expat’s Petitenget shop opens early with specialty coffee and plenty of seating in one of Seminyak’s main dining strips.

- **Area:** Petitenget / Seminyak
- **Vibe:** Bright, minimalist, coffee-first; busy at peak meal times
- **Hours:** Daily 6:30–19:00 ([Expat. Roasters Petitenget](https://expatroasters.com/pages/expat-roasters-seminyak-petitenget))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Expat+Roasters+Petitenget+St+Seminyak)

### Revolver Umalas — HQ & All Day Café

Revolver’s Umalas HQ is the brand’s all-day café on Jl. Bumbak Dauh — coffee, brunch and dinner in one building.

- **Area:** Umalas
- **Vibe:** Bustling café; best for email and planning, not all-day video calls
- **Hours:** Daily 7:00–22:00 ([Revolver Umalas](https://revolverbali.com/pages/umalas))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Revolver+Umalas+Jl.+Bumbak+Dauh+1+Umalas)

### Carmen Wine House

Carmen markets itself as a creative, smoke-free house with **power outlets at the tables** — more evening creative space than morning office.

- **Area:** Kerobokan (Jl. Baliku II)
- **Vibe:** Artsy and social; check their event nights before you plan a quiet deadline day
- **Hours:** Daily 17:00–23:00 (closed Nyepi and major holidays); later on Saturdays ([Carmen Wine House](https://www.carmenwinehouse.com/))
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Carmen+Wine+House+Jl.+Baliku+II+Kerobokan)

## How to choose coworking in Seminyak & Kerobokan

**1. Pick by commute, not hype.** Sunset Road jams at rush hour. A space five minutes from your villa beats a “prettier” one across town.

**2. Match the space to your call load.** Use a proper coworking pass on meeting-heavy days; save cafés for writing and admin.

**3. Ask about hosting hours vs member access.** Biliq and Bali Bustle advertise 24/7 member access but limited **hosting** hours for walk-ins — plan accordingly.

**4. Stack work with community.** Coworking meetups are an easy on-ramp to people in Bali. ## FAQ: Coworking Seminyak

**Is there good coworking in Seminyak proper?**
Yes. Biliq Seminyak is in the neighbourhood; most other spaces sit on Sunset Road, which is a short ride from Petitenget and Oberoi.

**What is the cheapest verified desk time?**
Ranjana lists private desk/table options from IDR 30,000 ([Ranjana coworking](https://ranjanacoffee.com/coworking-space-kerobokan/)). Biliq flexidesk bills from IDR 500 per minute ([Biliq Bali](https://www.biliqbali.com/)).

**Can I work from cafés only?**
For light days, yes — ROU, Expat. Roasters and Revolver Umalas are solid café options. For back-to-back calls, use a coworking space with phone booths or focus rooms.

**Where should I stay — Seminyak, Kerobokan or Umalas?**
Seminyak for restaurants and nightlife; Kerobokan and Umalas for quieter villa life with quick access to both Seminyak and Canggu. Our area guides above cover rents and hospitals.
