## Sanur for remote workers: coworking, cafes & the tech scene

Sanur is the part of Bali where mornings feel unhurried, the beach path runs for kilometres, and you can still join a video call without fighting scooter noise outside every window. If you are a **Sanur digital nomad** (or thinking about becoming one), the trade-off is simple: fewer coworking brands than Canggu, but more space, calmer traffic, and a tight-knit crowd once you show up a few times.

This guide lists coworking spaces, work-friendly cafes, and recurring tech or builder meetups in Sanur that we verified online as operating now. For rents, hospitals and day-to-day life, start with our [Sanur area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/sanur). When you are ready to meet people, browse [what is on in Sanur](https://balisquad.com/event/in/sanur) on BaliSquad.

### What working from Sanur feels like

Sanur suits remote workers who want a slower base without leaving Bali entirely:

- **Walkable pockets along Jalan Danau Tamblingan** put cafes, shops and the beach path within easy reach.
- **Coworking is concentrated, not crowded.** Two dedicated spaces cover most nomad needs; cafes fill the gaps on light days.
- **The community is smaller than Canggu's**, which makes recurring meetups easier to recognize—same faces, same Thursday table.

If you are comparing areas, see [Coworking in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/) for the busier west-coast scene, or [Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/) for cliff-side options in the south.

## Coworking spaces in Sanur

### Livit Hub

Livit Hub is a multi-floor hub in central Sanur, about a five-minute walk from the beach. Think ergonomic desks, phone and video rooms, a rooftop with wide views, an in-house kitchen cafe, and a coffee cart—more “campus” than a single open floor.

- **Where:** Jl. Bumi Ayu Gg. Pungut Sari No.6, Sanur ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Livit+Hub,+Jl.+Bumi+Ayu+Gg.+Pungut+Sari+No.6,+Sanur,+Bali))
- **Vibe:** Bright, social, good for founders and teams who like community programming; rooftop is popular for breaks and sunset chats.
- **Access:** [Livit’s site](https://liv.it/livit-hub-coworking-bali/) lists **24/7 access for members**; The Coffee Cart runs **Monday–Friday, 9 am–4 pm** (closed on public holidays).
- **Day pass (Basic):** IDR 140,000 (café and rooftop areas; prices inclusive of 11% VAT on their site)
- **10 days + weekends (Basic):** IDR 1,275,000
- **Monthly unlimited (Basic):** IDR 2,250,000
- **Pro day pass:** IDR 275,000 (flex desk anywhere in the building, quiet/nap room, more phone-booth time)

Best for: nomads who want amenities, events, and late-night access after a staffed first check-in.

### FLOW Workspace

FLOW sits on Jalan Danau Poso, close to the bypass and the beach side of Sanur. The layout mixes open collaboration zones, focus areas, phone booths, meeting rooms, and an outdoor terrace—aimed at freelancers and small teams who want a polished office feel.

- **Where:** Jl. Danau Poso No.66, Sanur ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=FLOW+Workspace,+Jl.+Danau+Poso+No.66,+Sanur,+Bali))
- **Vibe:** Professional and quiet; good when you need booths for calls and a clear “office” boundary.
- **Registration hours:** Monday–Friday **9 am–6 pm**, Saturday **9 am–5 pm**, Sunday closed; **registered members have 24/7 access** ([FLOW memberships page](https://www.flowworkspacebali.com/ourworkspaces))
- **Day pass:** IDR 300,000 (hot desks and standing desks, phone booths, access until midnight)
- **Weekly:** IDR 1,050,000
- **Monthly (FLOW Community):** IDR 3,800,000
- **10 days per month:** IDR 2,400,000

Best for: remote professionals who want 24/7 member access and meeting-room infrastructure without the Canggu commute.

## Laptop-friendly cafes in Sanur

On email-only days, a cafe beats a monthly pass. These spots are on Sanur’s main cafe strip or the beach—and we only list hours we could confirm from their own sites or consistent listings.

### Over the Moon Cafe

A large, air-conditioned cafe on Danau Tamblingan with a big menu and a steady laptop crowd—also the home base for weekly builder meetups (see below).

- **Where:** Jl. Danau Tamblingan No.19, Sanur ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Over+The+Moon+Cafe,+Jl.+Danau+Tamblingan+No.19,+Sanur))
- **Vibe:** Lively but workable; comfortable seating and AC for long sessions.
- **Hours:** **8:00 am–9:00 pm every day** ([overthemoonbali.com](https://www.overthemoonbali.com/))

Best for: half-day work blocks and meeting people over lunch.

### Medin Bali Cafe & Coffee

Mediterranean-leaning all-day dining on the same street—wood-fired pizza, breakfast plates, coffee—popular with locals and long-stay visitors.

- **Where:** Jl. Danau Tamblingan No.34, Sanur ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Medin+Bali+Cafe,+Jl.+Danau+Tamblingan+No.34,+Sanur))
- **Vibe:** Relaxed dining room; suited to writing and admin rather than all-day video marathons.
- **Hours:** **7:00 am–11:00 pm, Monday–Sunday** ([Bali Privilege Club partner listing](https://baliprivilegesclub.com/partners/medin-bali-cafe-coffee-sanur/), which publishes the cafe’s operating hours)

Best for: morning starts and evening work when coworking front desks are closed.

### Genius Cafe (Mertasari Beach)

A beachfront cafe that started as a coworking-friendly spot for entrepreneurs—sand, shade, and a health-focused menu at the quieter southern end of Sanur.

- **Where:** Mertasari Beach, Sanur ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Genius+Cafe+Mertasari+Beach+Sanur))
- **Vibe:** Open-air and scenic; best when your workload tolerates breeze and beach energy. The site advertises **fast, reliable Wi‑Fi** as part of the experience ([Genius Cafe Bali](https://geniuscafebali.com/)).
- **Hours:** **7:00 am–10:00 pm, Monday–Sunday** ([Genius Cafe Bali homepage](https://geniuscafebali.com/))

Best for: creative work with a view; pair with a proper coworking day when you have back-to-back calls.

Cafe etiquette still applies: keep ordering, take long calls outside or to a booth, and avoid peak brunch if you need a table for hours.

## Tech scene & recurring meetups in Sanur

Sanur’s builder crowd is smaller than Canggu’s, but it shows up on a schedule—especially on Thursdays.

### Hackanur (weekly)

Community-run coworking for indie makers, coders, designers, and hardware hackers building their own products. You work on your project alongside others, with intros around **9:30 am** and demos around **noon**.

- **When:** **Every Thursday, 9:00 am–12:00 pm** ([BaliSquad event listing](https://balisquad.com/event/hackanur-IPh6lD), [Miranesia series page](https://miranesia.com/bali/sanur/hackanur-2026-11-05-438efb3656d5))
- **Where:** Over the Moon Cafe, Sanur ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Over+The+Moon+Cafe,+Jl.+Danau+Tamblingan+No.19,+Sanur))
- **Cost:** Free (organizer-hosted; BaliSquad lists upcoming dates on the [Hackanur community page](https://balisquad.com/page/hackanur))

Best for: feedback on what you are shipping and meeting other builders without a formal conference.

### Sunset Networking at Livit Hub (biweekly)

Livit Hub’s **Sunset Networking** is a casual rooftop sundowner for founders, remote professionals, and nomads—drinks, conversation, and golden-hour views.

- **When:** **Every other Thursday, 5:30–6:30 pm** ([Luma event page](https://luma.com/aqt8runa))
- **Where:** Livit Hub rooftop ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Livit+Hub,+Jl.+Bumi+Ayu+Gg.+Pungut+Sari+No.6,+Sanur,+Bali))
- **Cost:** Free to register on Luma (confirm the next date before you go)

Best for: low-pressure networking after a focused workday.

## How to choose your Sanur work setup

**1. Match the venue to your calendar.** Call-heavy weeks → Livit or FLOW with phone booths. Light writing days → Medin or Genius on the beach.

**2. Test Wi‑Fi on a real meeting.** Use a day pass before committing to a month; upload speed matters more than download for video.

**3. Plan around staffed hours.** Both spaces gate **first-time registration** to weekday (and Saturday, for FLOW) front-desk hours even when members have 24/7 access.

**4. Stack cafe mornings with community evenings.** Hackanur’s Thursday morning rhythm pairs naturally with Sunset Networking on alternate weeks—check [Sanur events](https://balisquad.com/event/in/sanur) so you do not miss one-off workshops or pop-ups.

**5. Get around safely.** Sanur is flatter and more walkable than the Bukit, but scooters still help for grocery runs—see our [guide to renting a scooter in Bali](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/renting-a-scooter-in-bali/).

## FAQ: Remote work in Sanur

**Is Sanur good for digital nomads?**
Yes, if you want calmer streets, beach walks, and a smaller scene. You trade some variety for fewer distractions—and you will travel to Canggu or Ubud when you want a big night out.

**How many coworking spaces are there in Sanur?**
Two dedicated spaces we verified as operating: **Livit Hub** and **FLOW Workspace**. Cafes carry most casual laptop days.

**Are there tech meetups in Sanur?**
Yes. **Hackanur** meets weekly on Thursday mornings at Over the Moon Cafe. **Sunset Networking** runs every other Thursday evening at Livit Hub.

**Can I work from cafes only?**
For short sessions, absolutely. For daily video calls and backup power, a coworking pass is usually worth it.
