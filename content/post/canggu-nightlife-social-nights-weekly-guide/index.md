## Canggu nightlife & social nights: a weekly guide

Canggu after dark is loud, social, and spread across two main strips: **Berawa** (beach clubs and big-room venues along Jl. Pantai Berawa) and **Batu Bolong** (the walkable pub crawl from Old Man’s down to the sand). If you are new on the island, the tricky part is not finding *a* bar — it is knowing which night actually matches your vibe, and which plans are still running this month.

This guide covers **real bars, beach clubs, and recurring social nights in Canggu** that we could verify on [balisquad.com/event/in/canggu](https://balisquad.com/event/in/canggu), on each venue’s own site, or on BaliSquad event pages. We only grouped something under a weekday when a source says it repeats weekly. Line-ups change, so check the venue or BaliSquad before you dress up and book a ride.

For daytime laptop spots before you go out, see our [best laptop-friendly cafés in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/best-laptop-friendly-cafes-in-canggu/) guide. For rents, clinics, and getting around, start with the [Canggu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/canggu).

### Two strips, one scooter ride apart

**Berawa** is where you’ll find FINNS, MESA, Miss Fish, Desa Kitsuné, and The Shady Pig — dinner that turns into a dance floor, rooftop energy, and a dressier crowd. **Batu Bolong** is Old Man’s, Motel Mexicola, Luigi’s, and the beach-end bars — easier to bar-hop on foot, but traffic between the two pockets is slow after sunset. Pick one area per night unless you enjoy sitting in a GoCar.

Community-wise, Canggu’s “social night” scene is not only clubs. Board games, billiards, comedy, and welcome drinks pop up all week on [BaliSquad’s Canggu calendar](https://balisquad.com/event/in/canggu). That mix is why a lot of newcomers meet their first real friend group here instead of only at a club door.

## Beach clubs & all-day parties

### FINNS Beach Club

FINNS sits on the Berawa beachfront and runs **all-day parties with live DJs and singers every day of the week**, with free entry listed on its official site. It is the classic sunset-to-late warm-up before a Berawa club night, or a full day out on its own.

- **Where:** Jalan Pantai Berawa, Canggu
- **What to check:** [finnsbeachclub.com](https://www.finnsbeachclub.com/) for the current day’s programme and any ticketed specials

## How to plan a week without living in Stories

1. **Pick one club night and one community night.** A Berawa party plus a BaliSquad board game or billiards evening beats trying to do everything.
2. **RSVP on BaliSquad for community events.** You will see who else is going and get the exact address — handy when comedy or pop-ups move venues.
3. **Book dinner tables early** at Miss Fish, Luigi’s, or Mexicola on big nights; walk-ins work better midweek.
4. **Sort your ride home before midnight.** Batu Bolong beach pickups get messy late; share a ride or pre-book.

We wrote more about discovery habits in [How to Find Events in Bali (Without Living in WhatsApp Groups)](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/how-to-find-events-in-bali-without-living-in-whatsapp-groups/). ## FAQ: Canggu nightlife

**What is the main keyword area for Canggu nightlife?**
Most of the scene clusters in **Berawa** and **Batu Bolong**, with FINNS and MESA on the Berawa beach road and Old Man’s anchoring Batu Bolong.

**Is there a free beach club party in Canggu?**
FINNS advertises **free entry** and daily parties on [finnsbeachclub.com](https://www.finnsbeachclub.com/). Other venues may charge for daybeds or events — always check the official page.

**Where do newcomers meet people besides bars?**
[BaliSquad’s Canggu events](https://balisquad.com/event/in/canggu) list board games at Mata Berawa, billiards at The Green Felt, Toastmasters at Tropical Nomad, comedy nights, and hostel welcome drinks — often easier than cold-approaching a dance floor.

**Is Miss Fish open on Monday?**
No. Miss Fish’s FAQ lists **Tuesday to Sunday, from 6 pm until close**.

**Does every venue in this guide run the same DJ every week?**
No. Weekly *names* (like MESA’s themed nights or Old Man’s music residencies) stay on the calendar, but guest DJs and set times change. Confirm on the venue site or Instagram.

**How do I find what changed this week?**
Start at [balisquad.com/event/in/canggu](https://balisquad.com/event/in/canggu), then cross-check the venue’s own site for line-ups and door policies.
