## Gyms & fitness in Bali: where to train by area

If you are looking for a **gym in Bali**, you quickly learn the island is not one scene. Canggu is packed with CrossFit boxes, recovery clubs and fight gyms. Ubud skews yoga and functional fitness. Seminyak and Kerobokan have big commercial gyms and boutique studios. The Bukit is spread out, so picking something near where you sleep saves a lot of scooter time.

This guide lists **real gyms and fitness studios we checked on the web are operating now**, area by area. We focus on places with a clear address and an active website or official listing. We only include opening hours or pass prices when we could verify them on those sources. Lists change fast in Bali, so double-check before you buy a monthly pass.

For rents, food and the social side of each neighbourhood, see our area guides on [balisquad.com/guides](https://balisquad.com/guides). If you are still choosing a base, **gym Canggu** options are the densest on the island, but they are not the only good ones.

## Gyms and fitness in Canggu {#canggu}

Canggu is Bali’s busiest training corridor: heavy lifting, CrossFit, HYROX, MMA and class-based studios all within a short ride. Traffic between Berawa, Batu Bolong and Padonan can turn a five-minute map distance into twenty minutes at rush hour, so pick a gym you will actually go to, not the one with the best Instagram reel.

More on living here in our [Canggu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/canggu).

Day and membership pricing is on [bodyfactorybali.com](https://www.bodyfactorybali.com/). [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Body+Factory+Bali+Jl.+Nelayan+27+Canggu)

**Wanderlust Fitness Village** — CrossFit affiliate, HYROX, open gym, fight centre and a large class timetable. Jl. Raya Padonan No. 6, Canggu (their site also lists a Berawa address). Example pass prices on their site include a 1-day pass at IDR 350K. [wanderlustfitnessvillage.com](https://www.wanderlustfitnessvillage.com/). [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Wanderlust+Fitness+Village+Padonan+Canggu)

Hours on their site: Mon–Fri 6 am–10 pm, Sat–Sun 7 am–6 pm; example daily pass IDR 300K (promotional pricing may apply). [balimma.com](https://www.balimma.com/). ## How to choose (without wasting your first week)

**Match the gym to your routine, not your Airbnb pin.** A perfect map location you skip because of traffic is worse than a slightly farther gym you enjoy.

**Ask about drop-in rules for classes.** Fight gyms and CrossFit boxes often require induction or booking; commercial gyms are usually simpler.

**Recovery counts in the tropics.** Surf, scooter posture and humid heat beat you up. If you train hard, factor in sleep and a rest day; BaliSquad is full of people who learned that the hard way.

## FAQ: Gyms in Bali

**Where is the best gym in Canggu?**
It depends what you train. Body Factory and Wanderlust are popular for strength, classes and recovery. Bali MMA is the go-to for martial arts. The Canggu Studio is class-based rather than a full gym floor.

**Is there CrossFit in Bali?**
Yes. Affiliated or branded CrossFit training shows up in Canggu (Wanderlust), Ubud (CrossFit Ubud at Ubud.Fitness), Seminyak (CrossFit Seminyak) and Sanur (The Paradise Bali lists CrossFit).

**Are there 24-hour gyms in Bali?**
Fitness Plus branches (including Sanur, Sesetan, Jimbaran and several Uluwatu locations) advertise 24-hour access on their official branch pages.

**Do I need a scooter to get to the gym?**
In Canggu, Seminyak and Kerobokan you can sometimes walk or ride-share for one area, but most people use a scooter or car for consistency. See our [scooter rental guide](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/renting-a-scooter-in-bali/) if you are new to riding here.
