## Clinics & Hospitals in Bali: A Practical Guide by Area

Nobody plans to need a doctor in Bali, but surf cuts, scooter scrapes, stomach bugs and fevers are part of island life. When it happens, the last thing you want is to be searching Google Maps at 2 am.

This guide lists the main 24-hour hospitals and clinics near each area BaliSquad covers, with addresses you can save now. Save the emergency numbers to your phone today.

*This guide is general information, not medical advice. In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 or go straight to the nearest hospital emergency department.*

---

### Emergency numbers in Bali

| Service | Number |
|---|---|
| General emergency | **112** |
| Ambulance | **118** |
| Police | **110** |
| Fire | **113** |

You can also call a hospital's own emergency line directly. Some hospitals and clinics run their own ambulance services.

---

### Clinic or hospital?

- **Clinic:** minor cuts and wounds, stomach bugs, mild fevers, ear infections, prescriptions and check-ups. Many tourist areas have 24-hour clinics.
- **Hospital:** chest pain, trouble breathing, loss of consciousness, serious bleeding, head injuries, broken bones, or any serious scooter accident.

A good clinic will refer you to a hospital if you need one. If in doubt, go to the hospital.

## Clinics and hospitals near Canggu

Canggu has several 24-hour clinics for everyday issues. For anything serious, the nearest major hospitals are BIMC Kuta and Siloam on Sunset Road, about **30 to 45 minutes away** depending on traffic.

- **ER of Berawa:** a 24-hour emergency room. Berawa Beach Avenue, Tibubeneng. Tel +62 361 2100133.
- **Prime Plus Medical:** a 24/7 clinic. Jl. Subak Sari No.90A, Tibubeneng, with a second clinic at Jl. Sempol No.70a, Pererenan. Canggu clinic tel +62 361 4740055 or mobile +62 812 3738 7131.
- **Asta Medical Center:** a 24/7 clinic. Jl. Raya Canggu No.17R, Canggu. Tel +62 811 389 919.

## Hospitals near Kuta, Seminyak and Kerobokan

These two big private hospitals serve most of the west coast:

- **BIMC Hospital Kuta:** Jl. Bypass Ngurah Rai No.100X, Kuta. Open 24 hours. Tel +62 361 761263.
- **Siloam Hospitals Bali:** a private hospital covering all specialisations. Jl. Sunset Road No.818, Kuta. Main line +62 361 779900.

Rough travel times: about **10 to 15 minutes** from Kuta, **15 to 25 minutes** from Seminyak, and **20 to 30 minutes** from Kerobokan. From Kerobokan, BaliMéd in Denpasar is a similar distance (see Denpasar below).

Area guides: [Kuta](https://balisquad.com/guides/kuta), [Seminyak](https://balisquad.com/guides/seminyak), [Kerobokan](https://balisquad.com/guides/kerobokan).

## Clinics and hospitals near Uluwatu and the Bukit

Uluwatu is spread out, so know your nearest option before you need it. Local clinics handle minor injuries. For anything serious, head to BIMC Nusa Dua or Kasih Ibu Hospital in Kedonganan, **roughly 30 to 45 minutes away**.

- **911 Medical Clinic (Pecatu):** a 24/7 clinic for emergencies and urgent care. Jl. Pantai Bingin No.10, Pecatu. Tel +62 851-9591-1911.
- **BIMC Hospital Nusa Dua:** 24-hour accident and emergency centre. Kawasan ITDC Blok D, Nusa Dua. Tel +62 361 3000911.
- **Kasih Ibu Hospital Kedonganan:** a 100-bed general hospital, open 24 hours. Jl. Uluwatu No.69A, Kedonganan. Hotline (0361) 300 3030, WhatsApp +62 811 398 3030.

Surfing the reef breaks? Reef cuts get infected easily in the tropics. Clean them well and have them checked if they turn red or swollen.

More in our [Uluwatu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/uluwatu). If you work from the Bukit, see [Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/).

## Clinics and hospitals near Ubud

Clinics in Ubud handle everyday care. Major hospitals are in Denpasar, **about an hour away by car**.

- **BIMC Ubud Medical Centre:** doctors, nurses, an emergency room, pharmacy and lab, all 24 hours, plus an ambulance. Jl. Raya Sanggingan No.21, Kedewatan, Ubud. Tel +62 361 2091030.
- **Unicare Clinic Ubud:** a 24-hour clinic that also does doctor home visits. Jl. Raya Pengosekan No.88, Ubud. Tel and WhatsApp +62 822 9829 8911.

## Hospitals in Denpasar

Denpasar has the island's major hospitals. It is where serious cases from across Bali usually end up.

- **RSUP Prof. dr. I.G.N.G. Ngoerah** (formerly Sanglah): Bali's main government referral hospital, a Type A teaching hospital with a 24-hour emergency department. Jl. Diponegoro, Denpasar. Emergency tel (0361) 227911.
- **BaliMéd Hospital Denpasar:** a private hospital with a 24-hour emergency department. Jl. Mahendradatta No.57X, Denpasar. Emergency +62 855 390 6501.
- **Prima Medika Hospital:** 24-hour emergency department. Jl. Raya Sesetan No.10, Denpasar. Main line +62 361 236225.

## What to bring and how to prepare

- **Passport or a copy of it.** Hospitals will ask for ID.
- **Travel or health insurance details.** Save your insurer's 24-hour assistance number and policy number in your phone. Private hospitals often ask foreign patients for a deposit or card guarantee before non-emergency admission. Call your insurer's assistance line early and ask them to send the hospital a guarantee of payment.
- **A way to pay.** Have a card or cash ready.
- **A list of your medications and allergies.**
- **Someone's number.** Tell a friend or your villa host where you are going.

Save this page and your area's nearest hospital before you need it. Five minutes now saves a lot of panic later.

## FAQ: Clinics and hospitals in Bali

**What is the emergency number in Bali?**
Call 112 for any emergency, 118 for an ambulance, 110 for the police and 113 for the fire service.

**What is the nearest hospital to Canggu?**
The nearest major hospitals are BIMC Kuta and Siloam Hospitals Bali on Sunset Road, about 30 to 45 minutes away depending on traffic. For urgent care, there are 24-hour options in Canggu, including ER of Berawa.

**Is there a hospital in Uluwatu?**
The nearest hospitals are BIMC Nusa Dua and Kasih Ibu Kedonganan, roughly 30 to 45 minutes away. For urgent care nearby, 911 Medical Clinic in Pecatu is open 24/7.

**Where is the nearest hospital to Ubud?**
BIMC Ubud Medical Centre in Kedewatan has a 24-hour emergency room. The major hospitals are in Denpasar, about an hour away.

**Where are Bali's main hospitals?**
In Denpasar, including RSUP Prof. dr. I.G.N.G. Ngoerah (formerly Sanglah), BaliMéd and Prima Medika. In the south, BIMC Kuta, Siloam Hospitals Bali, BIMC Nusa Dua and Kasih Ibu Kedonganan cover the tourist areas.
