## Best cafes to work from in Uluwatu & the Bukit

The Bukit is not Canggu. Distances are longer, brunch lines are shorter, and a lot of “best cafe” lists still mix in places that closed seasons ago. If you are hunting **cafes in Uluwatu** where you can actually open a laptop, the sweet spot is usually a proper coffee shop on Jalan Labuansait, a garden cafe near Bingin, or a quiet bakery morning in Ungasan before the heat kicks in.

This guide lists **10 cafes that were operating when we checked** (November 2026) across **Pecatu, Bingin, Ungasan and Jimbaran**. For each one you get the area, what it feels like for work, hours only where we could verify them online, and a Google Maps link. For dedicated desks, backup power and meeting rooms, pair these with [Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/). New to the peninsula? Our [Uluwatu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/uluwatu) covers neighbourhoods and getting around, and [Hidden Gems of Uluwatu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/hidden-gems-of-uluwatu-discover-the-charm-of-bukit-ungasan/) is a softer look at Ungasan life between work blocks.

### Cafe etiquette on the Bukit

Most places here are genuinely welcoming, but you are often sharing space with families, surfers drying off, and sunset crowds. Order when you sit down and again if you are staying past a meal service, keep calls brief or step outside, and do not spread out across a big table at peak brunch if you are only nursing one coffee. When in doubt, ask staff where they prefer laptops — it saves awkwardness later.

## 1. BGS Uluwatu

**Area:** Pecatu (Jl. Labuansait, near the Suluban / Padang Padang turn)

BGS runs surf shops and coffee bars across Bali; the Uluwatu site sits on the main Labuansait strip where a lot of the Bukit’s cafe traffic passes. Their location page lists **free Wi-Fi**, outdoor seating, and opening hours.

- **Hours:** Daily 06:30–22:00 ([BGS Uluwatu location page](https://bgsbali.com/location/bgs-uluwatu-surf-shop-coffee-bar/))
- **Work vibe:** Early-start friendly and surf-casual. Good for a focused morning before the midday rush. Wi-Fi is advertised on the venue’s own site; still test it before an important call.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=BGS+Uluwatu+Jl.+Labuansait+Pecatu+Bali)

## 2. Suka Espresso (Uluwatu)

**Area:** Pecatu (Jl. Labuansait No. 10)

Suka is the Australian-style brunch cafe a lot of Bukit regulars default to: specialty coffee, all-day food, and a busy but comfortable dining room. The collective’s Uluwatu page lists daily hours and the Labuansait address.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:30–22:00 ([Suka Uluwatu — By/Suka Collective](https://www.bysuka.com/suka-uluwatu))
- **Work vibe:** Strong coffee and plenty of tables, but weekends fill up late morning. Better for email, writing and planning than a day of back-to-back video calls. Arrive earlier if you want a quieter table.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Suka+Espresso+Jl.+Labuansait+No.10+Pecatu+Bali)

## 3. Drifter Cafe Uluwatu

**Area:** Pecatu (Jl. Labuan Sait No. 52)

Drifter’s cafe sits behind the surf shop, with a garden feel and a menu built around whole-food breakfasts, lunches and dinners. The official cafe page lists service hours and the Pecatu address.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–22:00 (cafe and gelato; breakfast service until 5 pm, lunch and dinner from 12 pm) ([Drifter Cafe Uluwatu](https://driftersurf.com/pages/cafe))
- **Work vibe:** Relaxed and slightly tucked away compared with the street-front spots on Labuansait. Evenings can pick up when live music is on. A good pick for a half-day session and a proper meal break.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Drifter+Surf+Jl.+Labuan+Sait+No.52+Pecatu+Bali)

## 4. Brunch Station Uluwatu

**Area:** Pecatu (Jl. Labuansait No. 73, near Padang Padang)

Brunch Station is a small, surf-adjacent cafe a short walk from Padang Padang. The venue’s own site advertises daily hours and surfboard parking — handy if you are combining a session with a desk hour.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–19:00 ([Brunch Station Bali](https://brunch-station.com/))
- **Work vibe:** Compact and social; best for a morning block or post-surf brunch rather than an all-day office. Closes at 7 pm, so plan elsewhere for evening work.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Brunch+Station+Uluwatu+Jl.+Labuansait+73+Pecatu+Bali)

## 5. Milk & Madu Uluwatu

**Area:** Pecatu (Jalan Labuan Sait)

Milk & Madu’s Uluwatu branch is the big, family-friendly format the brand is known for: all-day food, indoor–outdoor seating and a kids’ playground. The brand’s locations page lists Uluwatu hours separately from Canggu.

- **Hours:** Daily 06:30–22:00 ([Milk & Madu locations — Uluwatu](https://milkandmadu.com/location/))
- **Work vibe:** Laptops are common, but so are families and sunset sessions. Pick a table away from the playground if you are on calls, and expect more background noise than at a dedicated coworking floor.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Milk+%26+Madu+Uluwatu+Jalan+Labuan+Sait+Pecatu+Bali)

## 6. Analog Uluwatu

**Area:** Pecatu (Jl. Labuansait)

Analog pairs specialty coffee with a listening-bar atmosphere — retro surf interiors, strong espresso, and a crowd that mixes Pilates classes with late breakfast. The Uluwatu site confirms the Pecatu venue; opening times vary by day on local listings.

- **Hours:** Monday 06:30–16:00; Tuesday–Sunday 06:30–23:00 ([The Bali Guideline — Analog Uluwatu](https://thebaliguideline.com/dine/uluwatu/analog-uluwatu))
- **Work vibe:** Mellow and design-forward. Monday closes mid-afternoon, so it suits early-week morning focus. Evenings shift toward bar energy — great for a debrief, less ideal for deep work.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Analog+Uluwatu+Jl.+Labuansait+Pecatu+Bali)

## 7. The Cashew Tree

**Area:** Bingin (Jl. Pantai Bingin No. 9, Pecatu)

The Cashew Tree is a garden cafe inland from the Bingin cliffs — healthy bowls, wraps, organic produce, and a play area that makes it popular with families by day. Thursday nights are louder, with live music listed on venue directories.

- **Hours:** Monday–Wednesday and Friday–Sunday 07:30–22:00; Thursday 07:30–23:30 ([The Bali Guideline — The Cashew Tree](https://thebaliguideline.com/dine/uluwatu/the-cashew-tree))
- **Work vibe:** Quietest on weekday mornings. Large tables and greenery make it easy to settle in for a few hours. Skip Thursday if you need silence.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Cashew+Tree+Jl.+Pantai+Bingin+No.9+Pecatu+Bali)

## 8. Banana Lounge

**Area:** Bingin (Tj. Simah, Pecatu)

Banana Lounge is a pool lounge and restaurant tucked near Bingin and Impossibles, with breakfast served daily and all-day food. The official site lists opening hours and the Pecatu address.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–23:00 ([Banana Lounge Bali](https://www.bananaloungebali.com/))
- **Work vibe:** Tropical and relaxed — poolside energy rather than a silent office. Breakfast hours (7–11 am on their breakfast page) are the most laptop-friendly window. Ask staff where they prefer remote workers during busy pool days.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Banana+Lounge+Bali+Bingin+Pecatu+Bali)

## 9. Nectar Specialty Coffee & Viennoiserie

**Area:** Ungasan (Jl. Merak III No. 3)

Nectar is a bakery cafe in residential Ungasan — croissants, cakes and specialty coffee away from the Labuansait traffic. Their official site lists weekly opening hours and the Merak III address.

- **Hours:** Wednesday–Monday 08:00–17:00; **closed Tuesday** ([Nectar Specialty Coffee & Viennoiserie](https://madeavanindra.wixsite.com/website))
- **Work vibe:** One of the quieter **cafes near Uluwatu** for a reading or writing morning. Indoor seating and a bakery counter — not a late-night option. Closed Tuesdays, so plan around that.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nectar+Specialty+Coffee+Jl.+Merak+III+No.3+Ungasan+Bali)

## 10. ST. ALi Jimbaran

**Area:** Jimbaran (Samasta Lifestyle Village)

Jimbaran sits between the airport and the Bukit — useful if you live bay-side or want a change of scene without driving to Canggu. ST. ALi’s Jimbaran page lists daily hours and the Samasta address.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–21:00 ([ST. ALi — Jimbaran Bali](https://stali.com.au/pages/st-ali-bali-jimbaran))
- **Work vibe:** Polished cafe with serious coffee and a shopping-village setting. Mornings are calmer; it is a practical option before errands or an afternoon at the beach.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ST.+ALi+Samasta+Lifestyle+Village+Jimbaran+Bali)

## When a cafe is not enough

Cafes are perfect for flexible days. They are harder when your calendar is full of video calls, you need 24/7 access, or you want one desk you can leave set up all month. That is when a coworking pass earns its keep.

On the Bukit, [BukitHub](https://www.bukithub.com) is an **independent coworking space** in Ungasan (not a BaliSquad venue) with a focus floor, meeting room and an active community calendar. BWork Uluwatu and Uluwatu Hub in Pecatu are larger all-in-one options. Compare day passes, monthly rates and tips in [Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/).

You will also need wheels — distances here reward a scooter you are comfortable on. See [Renting a scooter in Bali](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/renting-a-scooter-in-bali/) for licence and helmet basics.

## FAQ: Cafes in Uluwatu for laptop work

**What are the best cafes in Uluwatu for working?**
For verified hours and a spread of neighbourhoods, start with **BGS Uluwatu**, **Suka Espresso** and **Milk & Madu** on Labuansait, **The Cashew Tree** or **Banana Lounge** near Bingin, **Nectar** in Ungasan for a quiet bakery morning, and **ST. ALi Jimbaran** if you are bay-side.

**Do Bukit cafes have Wi-Fi?**
Some venues advertise it clearly — **BGS Uluwatu** lists free Wi-Fi on its own site. Many others do not publish connection details. Ask when you order and run a quick test before you rely on a cafe for a call.

**Which area is best for cafe working — Pecatu, Bingin or Ungasan?**
**Pecatu (Labuansait)** has the most choice and the most traffic. **Bingin** is greener and cliff-adjacent but spread out. **Ungasan** is quieter and more residential — pair a cafe morning with the slower rhythm we wrote about in [Hidden Gems of Uluwatu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/hidden-gems-of-uluwatu-discover-the-charm-of-bukit-ungasan/).

**Should I cafe-work or get a coworking pass on the Bukit?**
Use cafes for light days and variety. Choose coworking when you need predictable hours, backup power, meeting rooms or a community you see every week. Our [Uluwatu coworking guide](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/) breaks down the main spaces.
