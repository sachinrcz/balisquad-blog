## Supermarkets & groceries in Bali: shop by area

If you are hunting for a **supermarket in Bali**, the useful answer is almost always “near where you are staying.” Canggu and Kerobokan have the densest mix of full-size supermarkets, express branches and deli-style shops. Ubud and Sanur lean more local, with a few strong chains plus morning markets. The Bukit is spread out, so knowing one reliable store on your side of the cliff saves a lot of back-and-forth on a scooter.

This guide lists **supermarkets, grocers and markets we checked on the web are operating now**, grouped by the same areas as our [BaliSquad guides](https://balisquad.com/guides). We include opening hours only when an official store page listed them at the time of writing. Stock and brands change quickly, so treat this as a starting map, not a price list.

Looking for the biggest choice near the beach clubs and coworking cafés? **Supermarket Canggu** runs are the easiest on the island, but you do not need to live in Berawa to eat well.

## How to shop smarter (without overfilling your scooter basket)

**Match the store to your cooking style.** Imported cheese and wine shops are wonderful for a dinner party; Pepito or Frestive runs cover most weekly basics. Traditional markets shine for fresh produce and spices if you go early.

**Check hours on the day you go.** Bali loves public holidays and temple ceremonies; even 24-hour-style chains sometimes adjust staffing.

**Bring reusable bags.** Many counters still default to plastic; a foldable bag saves hassle at checkout.

## FAQ: Supermarkets in Bali

**Where is the best supermarket in Canggu?**
For a full weekly shop, Pepito Tibubeneng or Pepito Market Canggu are the usual anchors. Popular Market Deli Canggu leans gourmet and deli counters. Frestive Canggu is another large-format option on Pemelisan Agung.

**What is the main supermarket chain in Bali?**
Pepito (including Popular and Gourmet Market formats) has the widest branch network on the island’s official store list. Bintang Supermarket and Frestive also cover several tourist areas.

**Are there organic or health-food grocers?**
Bali Buda runs multiple shop-and-café locations, including Ubud, Kerobokan, the Bukit and Renon in Denpasar, with hours listed on [their locations page](https://balibuda.com/our-locations/).

**Can I rely on convenience stores instead of supermarkets?**
Indomaret and Alfamart are everywhere for snacks and basics, but for fresh meat, variety and household sizes you will still want a supermarket run every week or so.
