## How to Add Custom Questions on BaliSquad

Need dietary notes, skill level, or “how did you hear about us?” before people show up? Use **Custom Questions** on your event form. Guests answer when they register.

This section is optional. Leave it empty if a simple RSVP is enough.

Custom Questions live on the **create/edit form**, next to Ticket tiers. They are not under Manage Event tabs.

---

### On a new event

1. Open [Add Event](https://balisquad.com/event/add).
2. **Desktop:** scroll past **Ticket tiers** to **Custom Questions** → **Add Question**.
3. **Mobile:** Basics → Details → Settings → **Finish**, then after photos and Ticket tiers open **Custom Questions** → **Add Question**.
4. Fill the question and hit **Publish Event** when the rest of the listing is ready.

Questions with empty text are dropped on submit, so don’t leave blank rows.

---

### On an event you already have

1. Open the event → **Manage Event** → **Edit Event**.
2. Find **Custom Questions** (same place as on Add Event).
3. **Add Question** or edit existing ones.
4. Save with **Update Event** (published) or **Publish Event** (draft).

---

### What you can set per question

- **Question text**
- **Type:** Text, Select (dropdown), or Checkbox (multiple)
- **Options** for Select/Checkbox (keep at least two; use **Add Option** for more)
- **Required** if they must answer before registering
- Reorder by dragging (desktop) or using up/down (mobile)

On mobile, tapping a question opens **Edit Question** with **Done** or **Delete Question**.

---

### Tips

- Ask only what you’ll actually use at the door
- Prefer short required questions; long forms scare off RSVPs
- Pair with [ticket tiers](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/how-to-add-ticket-tiers-on-balisquad/) when price and a couple of questions both matter
