## Coworking in Ubud: where nomads actually work

Ubud is the slower, greener counterweight to the coast: rice fields, temples, yoga studios, and a remote-work crowd that tends to stay longer than a two-week visa run. You can open a laptop in plenty of cafés, but **coworking in Ubud** is still a smaller scene than Canggu, and a lot of blog posts recommend places that closed years ago.

This guide lists coworking spaces and work-friendly cafés in Ubud that we could confirm are operating, with honest notes on vibe and location. For rents, clinics and getting around, start with our [Ubud area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/ubud). When you are ready to meet people offline, browse [what is on in Ubud](https://balisquad.com/event/in/ubud) on BaliSquad.

---

### Before you start: cross off the old names

Ubud’s coworking map turned over more than most areas in Bali. Two closures still show up everywhere:

- **Hubud**, the space that put Ubud on the nomad map, merged into Dojo Bali in 2019 and **closed with Dojo in December 2022**. It is not coming back.
- **Outpost’s Canggu workspace** shut in April 2024, but **Outpost still runs two coworking locations in Ubud** (see below).

If a list still stars Hubud or “Outpost Canggu,” treat the rest of that article with suspicion. Before you buy a monthly pass, check the venue’s own site or Instagram for recent posts, or walk in on a day-pass trial.

## Coworking spaces in Ubud

### Outpost Ubud Nyuh Kuning (Mas / Nyuh Kuning)

Outpost’s original Ubud coworking, a short ride south of central Ubud near the Monkey Forest. Downstairs is an air-conditioned focus room; upstairs is open-air collaboration space, with private offices and video-call booths on the ground floor. One Bali cowork membership covers **both** Outpost Ubud sites.

- **Where:** Nyuh Kuning Road, Mas, Gianyar ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Outpost%20Ubud%20Coworking%2C%20Nyuh%20Kuning%20Road%2C%20Mas%2C%20Bali))
- **Access:** open 24 hours every day, including weekends ([Outpost FAQ](https://destinationoutpost.co/cowork-in-ubud/))
- **Day pass:** sold at the host desk on-site; current rates are not listed on Outpost’s public site, so check when you arrive or message them before you commit ([Outpost cowork FAQ](https://destinationoutpost.co/cowork/))
- **Community:** workshops, mixers and member events; first-time visitors can try **free coworking on Mondays** at this location ([Outpost promotions](https://destinationoutpost.co/outpost-promotions/))

Best for: nomads who want a structured coworking setup, call booths, and a social calendar without living in the Canggu traffic.

### Outpost Ubud Penestanan (Penestanan)

Coworking sits on the rooftop above Outpost’s Penestanan coliving: glass-walled focus zone, open-air desks, meeting room, video-call booths, and **pool access** included with cowork passes. It is next to Penestanan’s restaurant strip and a few minutes by scooter from central Ubud.

- **Where:** Jl. Penestanan, Sayan, Kecamatan Ubud, Gianyar 80571 ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Outpost%20Ubud%20Penestanan%2C%20Jl.%20Penestanan%2C%20Sayan%2C%20Ubud%2C%20Bali))
- **Access:** open 24 hours every day, including weekends ([Outpost Penestanan FAQ](https://destinationoutpost.co/cowork-in-ubud-penestanan/))
- **Day pass:** sold at the host desk on-site; check current rates at reception or via Outpost before you visit ([Outpost cowork FAQ](https://destinationoutpost.co/cowork/))

Best for: people who like the Penestanan village feel and want coliving, pool and rooftop work in one membership.

### Ubud.Space (central Ubud)

A compact, central coworking and coffee spot on the main road through town. The indoor room is air-conditioned; you can book meeting rooms by the hour and rent a monitor for the day. Pricing is posted clearly on their site.

- **Where:** Jl. Raya Ubud, Kecamatan Ubud, Gianyar 80571 ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ubud.Space%20Coworking%20and%20Coffee%2C%20Jl.%20Raya%20Ubud%2C%20Ubud%2C%20Bali))
- **Hours:** daily 9:00 to 23:30 ([Ubud.Space](https://ubud.space/))
- **Passes:** open space IDR 40K per hour or IDR 170K per day; meeting rooms and monitor rental priced separately on the site ([Ubud.Space](https://ubud.space/))

Best for: a drop-in day in the middle of Ubud, late sessions, or hourly work between errands in town.

### Ubud Co-Working (Andong / Petulu)

A standalone building north-east of central Ubud, beside Puri Andong Villas, with open desks, team tables, a quiet air-conditioned room, meeting rooms and a one-person silent room. **PAV Kitchen** on site serves lunch with a rice-field terrace, so you can work a full day without leaving the block.

- **Where:** Jl. Raya Andong No.92, Petulu, Kecamatan Ubud, Gianyar 80571 ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ubud%20Co-Working%2C%20Jl.%20Raya%20Andong%20No.92%2C%20Petulu%2C%20Ubud%2C%20Bali))
- **Hours:** daily 8:00 to 22:00 ([Ubud Co-Working](https://ubudcoworking.com/))
- **Passes:** day and monthly rates are not published on their website; call **+62 882-1030-0222** or check [Ubud Co-Working](https://ubudcoworking.com/) before you ride out

Best for: a proper desk setup and on-site food when you would rather skip the central Ubud lunch rush.

### Titi Batu Ubud Club (Mas)

Titi Batu is a family sports club with a **co-working lounge upstairs**, plus restaurant and Indigo coffee shop. Membership holders get lounge access; day passes cover club facilities (pool, courts, sauna and steam room) but **not** the gym unless you buy the gym add-on.

- **Where:** Jl. Cempaka, Kumbuh, Mas, Ubud (across Pelangi School) ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Titi%20Batu%20Ubud%20Club%2C%20Jl.%20Cempaka%2C%20Mas%2C%20Ubud%2C%20Bali))
- **Hours:** daily 8:00 to 22:00; gym from 7:00 ([Titi Batu](https://www.titibatu.com/))
- **Day pass:** IDR 190,000 (facilities pass without gym; one entry, no in-and-out) ([Titi Batu rates PDF](https://www.titibatu.com/_files/ugd/dd295c_a4353b3424ba480b88bdfa5be41f6dd4.pdf))

Best for: a work block followed by a swim or sauna when you want movement baked into the day, not another hour at a desk only.

## Laptop-friendly cafés in Ubud

On light days, a café is enough. These two are outside the pure coworking list but set up for laptop sessions; still buy regularly, take calls outside, and expect weekend peaks.

### Alchemy Ubud (Penestanan)

Long-running plant-based restaurant with upstairs and garden seating in Penestanan, a few minutes west of central Ubud across the Campuhan bridge.

- **Where:** Jl. Penestanan Kelod No. 75, Penestanan, Ubud ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alchemy%20Ubud%2C%20Jl.%20Penestanan%20Kelod%2075%2C%20Ubud%2C%20Bali))
- **Hours:** daily 7:30 to 21:30 ([Alchemy Bali](https://www.alchemybali.co/home))

Best for: a comfortable meal between work blocks when you are already in Penestanan or coming from Outpost next door.

### The Hidden Space (Singakerta)

A small café tucked toward the rice fields in Singakerta. The venue describes itself as set up for remote work: power at the tables and Wi-Fi they market for getting work done.

- **Where:** Jl. Dangin Labak, Singakerta, Ubud 80571 ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The%20Hidden%20Space%20Ubud%2C%20Jl.%20Dangin%20Labak%2C%20Singakerta%2C%20Ubud%2C%20Bali))
- **Hours:** Monday to Saturday 9:00 to 18:00; closed Sunday ([The Hidden Space](https://thehiddensapceubud.blog/))

Best for: a quiet half-day away from the main Ubud traffic, when you do not need a meeting room.

Café etiquette: keep one drink or snack on the go, do not camp a four-top during lunch rush, and use headphones on calls.

## How to choose where to work in Ubud

**1. Match the neighbourhood to your commute.** Central Ubud is walkable but congested at peak hours. Nyuh Kuning, Penestanan, Mas and Andong are short scooter rides away; factor that in before you buy a month somewhere “ten minutes on the map” that becomes forty in rain.

**2. Decide if you need 24/7 access.** Outpost’s Ubud locations advertise round-the-clock access for members. Ubud.Space stays open until 23:30. Ubud Co-Working and Titi Batu close in the evening.

**3. Test on a call day.** Use a day pass or an hourly block on a day with real meetings. Check upload speed, booth availability, and how loud the open floor gets at lunchtime.

**4. Ask about power cuts.** Ubud gets seasonal storms. Ask what backup power and internet look like before you prepay a month.

**5. Use work time to meet people.** Coworking events and community cafés are an easy on-ramp in Ubud because the crowd skews long-stay. If the coast is calling, compare notes with [Coworking in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/) and [Coworking in Uluwatu & the Bukit](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-uluwatu-and-the-bukit/). ## FAQ: Coworking in Ubud

**Is Hubud still open?**
No. Hubud closed with Dojo Bali in December 2022.

**How many Outpost coworking spaces are there in Ubud?**
Two: Nyuh Kuning (Mas) and Penestanan. One Bali cowork membership covers both.

**Can I cowork in central Ubud without a scooter?**
Yes. Ubud.Space is on Jl. Raya Ubud in town. Most other spaces in this guide are easier with a scooter or ride-hail.

**Is there 24/7 coworking in Ubud?**
Outpost advertises 24/7 access at both Ubud locations. Ubud.Space closes at 23:30.

**Can I work from cafés only?**
For email and writing, often yes. For back-to-back video calls and reliable backup power, a coworking pass is the safer default.

**Which area is best for remote work in Ubud?**
Penestanan and Nyuh Kuning suit people who want village calm near town. Central Ubud suits walkable errands. Andong and Mas work if your villa is already north or south of the centre.
