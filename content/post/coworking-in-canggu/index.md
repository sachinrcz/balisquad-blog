## Coworking in Canggu: Where to Work, What It Costs, and How to Choose

Canggu has the densest mix of cafés, coworking spaces and gyms in Bali, which is exactly why so many remote workers land here first. You can open a laptop almost anywhere. The harder part is finding a place with Wi-Fi that holds up on a video call, a backup when the power drops, and people you actually want to sit next to.

This guide covers the coworking spaces in Canggu that are open right now, what they cost, and how to pick one without wasting your first week. If you are still deciding where to stay, start with our [Canggu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/canggu) for rents, getting around and the nearest hospitals.

---

### Before you start: a lot of Canggu lists are out of date

Canggu changes fast, and many "best coworking" lists online still recommend places that have closed. Two big ones:

- **Dojo Bali**, the original Canggu coworking space near Echo Beach, closed in December 2022.
- **Outpost Canggu Coworking** closed its Canggu workspace in April 2024. Outpost still runs coworking in Ubud.

So before you commit to a monthly pass, check a space's own website or Instagram for recent posts. A quick walk-in visit tells you more than any review.

## Coworking spaces in Canggu

### BWork Canggu

BWork is one of the biggest names in Canggu coworking, with a garden pool, focus rooms, meeting rooms, a studio room, showers and lockers, plus an in-house café.

- **Where:** Jl. Nelayan No.9C, Canggu
- **Access:** coworking is open 24/7 for members; the café runs 8 am to 9 pm
- **Day pass:** Rp 299K (Rp 199K for Indonesian ID holders)
- **Weekend pass:** Rp 200K (Saturday and Sunday)
- **One week unlimited:** Rp 1,200K
- **One month unlimited:** Rp 3,850K, which also covers BWork Uluwatu
- **Non-peak monthly:** Rp 2,000K for 3 pm to 10 am access, which works well if your clients are in Europe or the US

Best for: people who want a full-service space with lots of amenities and a big community.

### Tropical Nomad

A long-running Canggu coworking space with a big open floor, air-conditioned rooms upstairs for quiet work, a garden and private offices.

- **Where:** Jl. Subak Canggu No.2, Canggu
- **Access:** open 24/7
- **Passes:** 7-hour pass IDR 200K, full day pass IDR 260K, weekend pass IDR 350K (includes coffee)
- **Monthly:** Standard 50 hours IDR 1.35M, Power 100 hours IDR 2.2M, Unlimited IDR 3.3M, dedicated desk IDR 3.8M, or a Night Owl plan for evening workers at IDR 1.7M
- **Front desk:** 7 am to 9 pm on weekdays and 7 am to 5 pm on weekends, so sign up during those hours

Best for: a classic, focused coworking setup with room to spread out.

### PUCO Rooftop

A locally owned rooftop coworking space and eatery in Tibubeneng, with rice-field views, focus rooms and rooms downstairs if you want to live and work in one place.

- **Where:** Gg. Pratama No.1A, Tibubeneng (Canggu side)
- **Hours:** 24 hours Monday to Friday; 8 am to 10:30 pm on weekends
- **Dedicated desk:** Rp 2,500K per month, or Rp 2,900K with an adjustable desk and ergonomic chair
- **Focus room:** Rp 30K per hour
- **Day passes:** IDR 150K for 8 hours, IDR 200K for 24 hours (with a coffee or tea and an hour in a focus room), or IDR 100K for an evening pass from 5 pm
- **Hot desk:** IDR 750K per week or IDR 2,000K per month

Best for: a quieter, more local feel at a lower monthly price.

### AT 06 Bali

A locally owned hub that mixes a restaurant, coworking, a gym and community events, including Balinese culture nights and art exhibitions.

- **Where:** Jl. Batu Mejan No.6, Canggu
- **Day pass deal:** spend IDR 150K on food and drinks and your day pass is free; coworking members get 10% off food and drinks
- **Setup:** fast Wi-Fi, an air-conditioned indoor floor upstairs, and Skype and meeting rooms
- **Membership prices and hours:** check [AT 06 on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/at06.bali/) for the latest

Best for: people who like to work, train and eat in one spot.

## Laptop-friendly cafés in Canggu

Most cafés in Canggu double as workspaces, and on a light day that is all you need. A few with a coworking setup or a reputation for working crowds:

- **Awan Connection** (café and coworking space), Jl. Kayu Manis, just behind Pepito supermarket in Batu Bolong. Coworking is free with any café purchase, and it is closed on Sundays. Hours vary by day, so check [their Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/awanconnection/) before you go. It also hosts community events on BaliSquad, like its Deep Talks evenings, so it is an easy way to meet people while you work.
- **NOAH cafe**, Jl. Pantai Pererenan No.60, Pererenan. Open daily 7 am to 10 pm, with a quiet air-conditioned room for laptop work downstairs and a more social open-air floor upstairs. It fills up fast, so arrive early.

Café etiquette that keeps everyone happy: order something every couple of hours, take long calls outside, and avoid weekend brunch peaks if you need a table for hours.

## How much does coworking in Canggu cost?

Our [Canggu guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/canggu) puts a monthly coworking pass at roughly **IDR 2.5M to 4.5M**, and the spaces above fit that range:

| Option | Typical price |
|---|---|
| Day pass at a full-service space | around Rp 300K (BWork) |
| Dedicated desk at a smaller local space | Rp 2.5M to 2.9M per month (PUCO) |
| Unlimited monthly at a large space | around Rp 3.85M (BWork, includes Uluwatu) |
| Off-peak monthly (afternoon to morning) | around Rp 2M (BWork) |

Prices change, and many spaces run promos, Indonesian ID rates or free trial days. Always check the space's own site before you pay.

## How to choose a coworking space in Canggu

**1. Pick by neighbourhood, not by Instagram.** Canggu traffic peaks around sunset and on the shortcut roads. A space ten minutes from your villa beats a "better" one that takes forty minutes each way. Berawa and Batu Bolong are the busiest pockets; Pererenan is calmer.

**2. Test it on a call day.** Use a day pass on a day with real meetings. Check the upload speed, whether there are phone booths or focus rooms, and how loud the main floor gets.

**3. Ask about backup power and internet.** Power cuts happen in Bali. Ask what happens to Wi-Fi when the power drops.

**4. Match the hours to your time zone.** If you work US or European hours, you need 24/7 access. BWork, Tropical Nomad and PUCO (weekdays) all offer late-night or round-the-clock access.

**5. Look at the community, not just the desk.** Events are what turn a desk into a social life. Check what is on at each space and around Canggu on our [Canggu events page](https://balisquad.com/event/in/canggu).

## FAQ: Coworking in Canggu

**Is there 24/7 coworking in Canggu?**
Yes. BWork Canggu and Tropical Nomad advertise 24/7 access, and PUCO Rooftop is open 24 hours on weekdays.

**How much is a coworking day pass in Canggu?**
At a full-service space like BWork it is around Rp 299K. Smaller local spaces are often cheaper, so check each space's site.

**Is Dojo Bali still open?**
No. Dojo Bali closed in December 2022, and Outpost closed its Canggu coworking space in April 2024.

**Can I just work from cafés?**
For light days, yes. For calls and long sessions, a coworking pass gives you steadier Wi-Fi, backup power and quiet rooms.

**Which part of Canggu is best for remote work?**
Choose by commute. Stay close to your workspace to avoid the sunset traffic. Berawa and Batu Bolong are lively; Pererenan is quieter.
