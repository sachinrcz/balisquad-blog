## Best laptop-friendly cafes in Canggu

You do not need a coworking membership to get work done in Canggu. On a light day, a good cafe with stable Wi-Fi, a table you can keep for a few hours, and coffee that does not taste like regret is enough.

The trick is picking the right pocket of town and the right time of day. Brunch rush at Berawa is a different beast from a quiet mid-morning in Pererenan. This guide lists **10 cafes in Canggu that are open now**, with areas, hours we could verify online, and honest notes on what each place feels like for laptop work. For dedicated desks, backup power, and call booths, pair these with our [Coworking in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/) guide. New to the area? Our [Canggu area guide](https://balisquad.com/guides/canggu) covers neighbourhoods, transport, and clinics.

### Cafe etiquette that keeps you welcome

Canggu runs on an unspoken contract: order regularly, take long calls outside or in a corner, and do not camp the best four-top through Saturday brunch if you are not eating. Most places are genuinely laptop-friendly; staying welcome is mostly about reading the room.

---

## 1. Awan Connection

**Area:** Batu Bolong (Jl. Kayu Manis, behind Pepito supermarket)

Awan is a cafe, restaurant, and **free coworking space** in one building. The official site describes a quiet coworking area plus cafe seating, and coworking stays free when you buy food or drinks.

- **Hours (cafe & resto):** Monday–Tuesday 10:00–22:00; Wednesday–Saturday 10:00–17:00; **closed Sunday**
- **Hours (coworking):** Monday–Tuesday 10:00–22:00; Wednesday–Saturday 10:00–19:00; **closed Sunday**
- **Work vibe:** Best when you want a proper work zone without a day pass. Cafe side works for calls; the dedicated coworking floor is quieter. Closed Sundays, so plan around that.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Awan+Connection+Jl.+Kayu+Manis+Canggu+Bali)

Awan hosts community events on BaliSquad too, including Deep Talks evenings, so it doubles as an easy way to meet people after you close the laptop.

## 2. ZIN Cafe & ZIN@Work

**Area:** Nelayan (near Canggu Beach, Jl. Nelayan No. 78)

ZIN spreads across open-air dining areas and runs **ZIN@Work**, a free coworking space next door with bamboo-floor seating, an air-conditioned focus room, and **fast Wi-Fi** listed on their site. Late-night workers note the cafe side stays open well past most brunch spots.

- **Hours:** Daily 06:30–02:00 (last food orders 01:30, per [zin.world](https://zin.world/zin-cafe/))
- **Work vibe:** Strong pick for long sessions and evening work. More social than a pure coworking floor, but the separate ZIN@Work area gives you a clearer “office” mode.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ZIN+Cafe+Jl.+Nelayan+78+Canggu+Bali)

## 3. NOAH cafe

**Area:** Pererenan (Jl. Pantai Pererenan)

NOAH is a two-level cafe that draws a steady remote-work crowd. Downstairs mixes indoor and outdoor seating; upstairs is open air. Our [coworking guide](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/) calls out the **air-conditioned indoor area downstairs** for laptop sessions on hot days.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–22:00 (listed on multiple current directory listings; confirm on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/noahcafe.bali/) before you go)
- **Work vibe:** Arrive earlier if you want the cooler indoor tables. Evenings get busier. Wi-Fi is commonly listed for this venue on Google-based directories.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=NOAH+cafe+Jl.+Pantai+Pererenan+No.60+Canggu)

## 4. nüde cafe Berawa

**Area:** Berawa (Jl. Pantai Berawa)

nüde is an all-day cafe with a big breakfast and brunch menu and plenty of table seating. The Berawa location page on their site lists daily hours and the Pantai Berawa address.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–23:00 ([nüde Berawa location page](https://nude-bali.com/locations/berawa))
- **Work vibe:** Relaxed cafe energy rather than a silent office. Good for email, writing, and planning. Expect more background noise around lunch; Wi-Fi is listed on public venue listings for this address.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=n%C3%BCde+cafe+Berawa+Jl.+Pantai+Berawa+No.86)

## 5. BGS Canggu

**Area:** Echo Beach / Munduk Catu (Jl. Munduk Catu No. 1)

BGS is a surf-lifestyle coffee shop a short walk from Echo Beach. The official store page advertises **free Wi-Fi**, outdoor seating, card payment, and opening from early morning.

- **Hours:** Daily 06:30–22:00 ([BGS Canggu](https://bgsbali.com/store/bgs-canggu/))
- **Work vibe:** Casual and breezy. Mornings fill with the pre-surf crowd; afternoons are often easier for a longer session. Outdoor seating dominates, so bring sun protection and plan around heat and rain.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=BGS+Bali+Canggu+Jl.+Munduk+Catu+No.1)

## 6. Crate Cafe

**Area:** Pererenan (Jl. Sempol No. 8)

Crate is a long-running Canggu community cafe. It **moved to Pererenan** (Jl. Sempol); ignore old Padang Linjong pins still floating around online. The site lists long daily hours and an open, social layout.

- **Hours:** Daily 06:00–21:00 ([lifescrate.com](https://lifescrate.com/))
- **Work vibe:** Communal tables up front, quieter corners toward the back. Great for a focused morning before the lunch wave. Wi-Fi is not highlighted on their website, so run a quick speed test before you settle in for a video call.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Crate+Cafe+Jl.+Sempol+No.8+Pererenan+Bali)

## 7. Body Factory Cafe

**Area:** Nelayan (Jl. Nelayan No. 27, street front of the gym)

Body Factory’s street-side cafe serves specialty coffee alongside protein shakes, snacks, and health-focused drinks. Industrial, minimal interior; opens early.

- **Hours:** Daily 06:00–22:00 ([Body Factory cafe page](https://www.bodyfactorybali.com/cafe))
- **Work vibe:** Straightforward and unpretentious. Handy if you train at the gym and want a desk nearby, or if you like starting work at 6 am when Berawa is still quiet. Not a “linger all day” social hub like ZIN or Milk & Madu.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Body+Factory+Cafe+Jl.+Nelayan+No.27+Canggu)

## 8. Hungry Bird Coffee Roaster

**Area:** Tibubeneng / Canggu Shortcut (Jl. Raya Semat No. 86)

Hungry Bird is a **specialty coffee roaster** first and a cafe second. Asian Coffee Map lists consistent **07:00–17:00** daily hours and a no-fuss setting focused on espresso and filter coffee.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–17:00 ([Asian Coffee Map, verified May 2026](https://asiancoffeemap.com/reviews/bali/hungry-bird-coffee-roaster))
- **Work vibe:** Ideal for a short, serious coffee-and-deep-work block in the morning. Closes at 5 pm, so it is not an evening option. Some visitors report laptop policies can vary by how busy it is; ask staff when you order if you plan a long session.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hungry+Bird+Coffee+Roaster+Jl.+Raya+Semat+No.86+Canggu)

## 9. Milk & Madu Berawa

**Area:** Berawa (Jl. Pantai Berawa No. 52)

Milk & Madu brings a big, family-friendly Australian cafe format to Berawa: all-day food, cocktails later in the day, and a **playground** for kids. The brand’s location page lists Berawa hours and address.

- **Hours:** Daily 07:00–22:00 ([Milk & Madu locations](https://milkandmadu.com/location/))
- **Work vibe:** Wi-Fi and laptops are common here, but so are families and sunset sessions. Choose a table away from the playground if you are on calls, and expect more noise than at Awan or ZIN@Work.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Milk+%26+Madu+Berawa+Jl.+Pantai+Berawa+No.52)

## 10. The Shady Shack

**Area:** Tanah Barak / rice fields near Batu Bolong (Jl. Tanah Barak No. 57)

The Shady Shack is a vegetarian and vegan cafe set in a garden with rice-field views. The official site lists **Monday–Sunday 08:00–22:00** and takes reservations for larger groups.

- **Hours:** Daily 08:00–22:00 ([theshadyshackbali.com](https://www.theshadyshackbali.com/)); closed on Nyepi and Galungan
- **Work vibe:** Calmer and greener than the main Batu Bolong strip. Better for writing and admin than back-to-back video calls. Their site does not advertise Wi-Fi; ask staff if you need to get online.
- **Maps:** [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=The+Shady+Shack+Jl.+Tanah Barak+Canggu)

## When a cafe is not enough

Cafes are brilliant for flexible days. They are less brilliant when your calendar is full of video calls, you need guaranteed backup power, or you want one predictable desk five days a week. That is when a coworking pass pays for itself. Compare spaces and prices in [Coworking in Canggu](https://balisquad.com/blog/p/coworking-in-canggu/).

If your real goal is not just a desk but **people**, swap one solo cafe day a week for something on the calendar. ## FAQ: Best cafes in Canggu for Wi-Fi and laptops

**What are the best cafes in Canggu for Wi-Fi?**
For Wi-Fi called out by the venue itself, start with **BGS Canggu** (free Wi-Fi on their site) and **ZIN Cafe / ZIN@Work** (fast Wi-Fi listed for the coworking area). **Awan Connection** pairs cafe Wi-Fi with a free coworking zone when you buy food. Always test the connection on arrival before an important call.

**Do Canggu cafes allow laptops all day?**
Most do, within reason. Order every couple of hours, avoid hogging prime tables at weekend brunch, and take calls outside when you can.

**Which area of Canggu is best for cafe working?**
**Berawa** and **Batu Bolong** have the most choice but more traffic and noise. **Pererenan** and **Nelayan** are often calmer. Pick somewhere close to where you stay so you are not fighting sunset traffic twice a day.

**Are cafe hours in Bali reliable online?**
Hours change, especially around holidays. Check the venue’s Instagram or website the morning you go. This post lists sources and dates where we verified hours; when in doubt, message the cafe directly.
